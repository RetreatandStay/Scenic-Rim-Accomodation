The Kalbar Duplex

Built for crews. Just as good for a holiday.

Two self-contained units side-by-side, each with its own full kitchen, sleeping up to 4 people. Off-street parking easily fits work vehicles, and each stay includes a complimentary gym membership.

Close to Kalbar township, an easy walk to shops and takeaway. Minimum stay is 7 nights for all bookings, whether you're housing a crew for a project or taking a longer break in the Scenic Rim.

Complimentary gym membership included

Every stay comes with a complimentary membership to Boonah Health Hub, a fully equipped 24/7 gym in nearby Boonah, at no extra cost for the length of your stay. Members get full access to the strength and cardio floor, plus circuit, reformer Pilates and other group fitness classes. The Hub also runs on-site allied health services, including physiotherapy, exercise physiology and remedial massage, handy if a project or a big day out leaves you needing to work out the kinks.
